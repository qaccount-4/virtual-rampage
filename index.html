VR Brawler: Gesture Tester

Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and press Enter VR. Wear your wrist straps and clear your space.

No buttons: grab the ground or walls and push to move, like Gorilla Tag · in mid-air, push a hand down hard for an air platform (3 per jump)

Trigger + punch forward = jab (4th jab knocks back) · punch up = uppercut · punch down = ground pound · Touch fists together (no buttons held) = block bubble · B/Y = reset dummy · A/X = dummy AI off / easy / medium / hard (or throw the sword while holding it)

Sword: floats above one of 3 spots on the stage (a new one appears 3-5 s after it's gone), squeeze grip next to it to take it · jab/side slash: 3 hits then knockback (swing while moving = dash, 3 per combo) · up slash: hit, then knockback · down slash: bounces it off the floor · combos reset 1 s after your last swing

Grip + punch forward = special jab (its momentum carries you forward a little) · punch up = special uppercut · punch down = special slam (hops you up, stuns 1 s) · jab while moving fast = running jab (1 s stun, speeds you up)

On a computer: 1 jab 2 uppercut 3 ground pound 4 running jab 5 special jab 6 special uppercut 7 special slam · R resets dummy · F dummy AI level · G sword on/off (then 1-4 are sword attacks)
